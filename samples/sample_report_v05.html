Policy note (AI-assisted draft): growth, jobs and rural water

India's real GDP grew by 7.8% in FY 2025-26, according to MoSPI's National Accounts Statistics 2026 (https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/aug/doc_9748_20260831_15530601.pdf).1 Nominal GDP reached ₹345.37 lakh crore in 2025-26.2

In 2024-25, real GDP growth moderated to 6.5%, weighed down by weak manufacturing.3

The unemployment rate fell to 4.9% in 2023-24, and Telangana's unemployment rate was 4.1% in the same year.4 Female labour force participation rose from 37.0% in 2022-23 to 41.7% in 2023-24, an increase of 4.7%.5

PLFS has been conducted by MoSPI since 2017-18.6 In absolute terms, the fiscal deficit for 2024-25 was ₹157.4 billion.7

Jal Jeevan Mission was launched by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 15 August 2019 [1].8 Today 92% of rural households have tap water connections, up from 3.23 crore households at launch [1].9 The Union Government has extended Jal Jeevan Mission till December 2028.10

In March 2026 the Government introduced a Model Context Protocol server that links AI tools with official statistics; its beta version covered 12 statistical products [2].11 The Output PPI series that now deflates the IIP was released by the Reserve Bank of India on 15 June 2026.12

A recent meta-analysis found that piped water cut child diarrhoea by 30% (Sharma et al., 2024, doi:10.5555/jjm.fake.42).13 Statistical computing in Python builds on array programming with NumPy (Harris et al., 2020, doi:10.1038/s41586-020-2649-2).14 The Reserve Bank of India is headquartered in Mumbai.15

Policymakers should prioritise service delivery and source sustainability over new pipelines.16
